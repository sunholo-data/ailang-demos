# Woodland study

The habitat is a sunlit miniature seen from above: tactile moss, fern canopies,
scattered seedpods and small, soft-bodied animals. Real model judgments remain
visually distinct from atmospheric detail. Vegetation is scenery, not a claim
about the creature's perception or collision geometry.

Palette: deep spruce #243d35, shaded moss #526849, young fern #a4ba74,
seed husk #d3b984, chalk #e9eee5, lilac #a698b7.
Avenir Next handles headings and controls, with tabular numbers for evidence.

Layout: a generous uninterrupted woodland alongside a restrained observation
column. Creature names appear on hover/selection; no permanent red rings.

Avoid the generic flat garden of repeated grass symbols. Use rendered form,
contact shadows, irregular botanical silhouettes and clear quiet space around
creatures. Blender assets are pre-rendered, not a second live 3D engine.
