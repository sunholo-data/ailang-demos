Instinct, with evidence.
Things in this world have descriptions, not “edible” or “dangerous” flags. In live mode, a decision model reads a creature’s perception and answers typed questions with probabilities.
AILANG checks threats and the creature’s confidence threshold, then samples an action. The full decision and random roll are saved so that the action can be reproduced offline.
The habitat runs in your browser. Your browser sends new judgments directly to Jev through OpenRouter. You can inspect or replay a saved decision from the ledger without making another model call.


Give them intuition.
Your browser sends your key and creature perceptions directly to OpenRouter over HTTPS for TypeSafe Jev. Optional item pictures also use OpenRouter. No Sunholo server receives your key.
The key is saved in this browser. Live calls incur usage charges. This tab pauses at $0.10 of reported usage; a final call can cross that amount. Reset or reload starts a new session. This is an app limit, not an account-level spending cap. Recorded cost is shown in History.
Use an OpenRouter key with access to TypeSafe Jev; optional artwork uses FLUX.2 Klein.

Jev’s interpretation
Confidence and threat settings guide its choices; urgency can override caution. Speed and stamina change its movement and tiredness.
All Nouls can walk, investigate, eat, drink, poo, rest, flee, seek company and give each other space. Flying, magic, combat and crafting remain description only. Appearance uses one of our four existing creatures.
Inspect the mapping evidence
Numeric traits use the expected value of Jev’s full score distributions. Appearance uses its highest-ranked choice. Actions in the habitat are still sampled.
