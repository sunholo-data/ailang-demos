What runs when I make a sound?

AILANG checks and renders an original recipe, applies a short gain ramp and optional feedback delay, protects the sample peak, and encodes the WAV. Measurements describe the decoded output file. The echo tail is deliberately limited to a quarter second.

No sound is uploaded. Playback has the same additional quiet audition gain for both versions; the WAV keeps its prepared levels. These sample checks do not establish physical latency, perceived loudness or musical quality.
