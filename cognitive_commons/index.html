        What do the axes mean?
        

          
← critical

          
Interrogates assumptions, names costs, questions the value of what's being proposed. Skeptic territory.

          
forward →

          
Reaches toward a future state, an architecture, a possibility not yet built. Visionary territory.

          
↑ synthesizing

          
Reconciles opposing views, integrates contradictions, finds the unifying frame. Synthesizer territory.

          
↓ archival

          
Preserves history, records precedent, grounds the discussion in what's already been said. Archivist territory.

    What just happened
    
      
    Each tab is an isolated WASM-runnable cell with its own scoped DOM region. Stanzas are typed patches dispatched through the !: DOM effect; cross-tab gossip is the !: Msg effect carried by BroadcastChannel. Patches use content-hashed node IDs so the same sequence of events always produces the same HTML.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Why this is different
    
      
    Most multi-agent demos are non-deterministic, server-bound, and unreplayable. This one runs entirely in your browser, has no server, and reconstructs byte-identically from a JSONL log — even when authored by a stochastic LLM. The AI's output is just another event.
